#### Open Source Games I'm Working On
Here are *open source* games I'm working on with Godot and TMC's [Dot](https://moddingcommunity.com/co/4-dot-assets) collection. These are mostly for demonstration purposes and to show what TMC's gaming platform can do with its CDN and cross-platform play (web browser, PC/desktop app, and mobile). That said, these games are nowhere close to complete!

* [**G2gfast**](https://github.com/gamemann/game-g2gfast): A 3D-based game where players *surf* and *bunny hop* on *maps* to compete for world records. This is inspired off of Source Engine movements and Counter-Strike Surfing and Bunny Hopping.
* [**Playground**](https://github.com/gamemann/game-playground): A 3D-based game that acts as a sandbox. Players can join, spawn props, throw them around, and more. This game is inspired off of the game [Garry's Mod](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4000/Garrys_Mod/).
* [**Arena**](https://github.com/gamemann/game-arena): A 3D-based game where players rack up the most points to win individually or for their team in game modes such as deathmatch, gungame, and more.
* [**Hungario**](https://github.com/gamemann/game-hungario): A 2D-based game where players start off as small monsters and must eat food and others. The largest monster wins. Inspired off of the game [Agario](https://agar.io).
* [**Buses From Hell**](https://github.com/gamemann/mg-buses-from-hell): A 3D-based game where there are one or two bus driver(s) and the rest of the players must survive. The bus driver's goal is to eliminate all players. Inspired off of the classic [Counter-Strike: Source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/240/CounterStrike_Source/) map/mod, [buses_from_hell](https://gamebanana.com/mods/127607).
* [**Smash Copter**](https://github.com/gamemann/mg-smash-copter): A 3D-based game where all players spawn on-top of platforms being held up by a single pillar. There is a cannon in the middle that launches props into the air and if they fall onto a platform, they can make it collapse resulting in any players standing on the platform potentially falling to their death. If more than one survivor or team is alive by the time the on-screen timer ends, all players are teleported with weapons for final death. This map is highly inspired off of classic [Counter-Strike: Source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/240/CounterStrike_Source/) map/mod called [**mg_3k_smash_lego_copter**](https://gamebanana.com/mods/127650).
